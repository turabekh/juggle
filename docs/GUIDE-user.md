# Parent Guide — kidspots.store

**Who this is for:** parents and guardians booking kids' activities — school-holiday
programs, weekend & after-school one-offs, and birthday parties — in and around Perth.

This guide walks through everything you can do on the site, in the order you'll usually do
it: create an account, add your kids, find activities, book and pay, then manage your
bookings. Prices are always shown **GST-inclusive** — the amount you see is the amount you pay.

> **Terminology:** we call every account holder a **guardian**. A guardian can add several
> children and can share their family with a **co-guardian** (e.g. a partner) so you both see
> the same kids and bookings.

---

## 1. Getting started

### Create an account
1. Go to **kidspots.store** and choose **Sign up**.
2. Enter your **email**, a **password** (at least 8 characters), and your **full name**.
3. You're signed in immediately — no email confirmation step.

### Sign in
- **Email + password** — the standard login.
- **Text me a code (SMS)** — if phone sign-in is enabled, enter your mobile number and we'll
  text you a one-time code. Verifying the code signs you in and, if it's a new number, creates
  an account for you. Codes are rate-limited for your protection, and we never reveal whether a
  number is already registered.

If you ever forget which method you used, both lead to the same account as long as they're
linked to the same details.

### Add your children ("My kids")
Open the avatar menu (top right) → **My kids** (the Account page).

For each child, add:
- **First name** (first name only — we never collect surnames or photos),
- **Date of birth** (used for age-matching to sessions),
- **Medical / allergy notes** and an **emergency contact**.

Why we ask: we collect the **minimum** needed to keep your child safe at an activity, nothing
more. This information is shown only to the provider running a session your child is booked
into, on their roster.

### Share your family with a co-guardian
On the Account page you can invite a **co-guardian** by email:
1. **Invite** — enter the email of someone who already has a kidspots account.
2. They **accept** the invitation from their own account.
3. From then on you both see the same children and bookings.

You can remove a co-guardian link at any time. (The person you invite must already have an
account — invite them to sign up first if they don't.)

### Deleting your account
The Account page has a **delete account** option. This permanently removes your account and
your family's personal data (an Australian Privacy Principles data-erasure request). It can't
be undone.

## 2. Finding activities

### Search (Discover)
The **Discover** page is your main search. Filter by:

| Filter | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Location** | Suburb, postcode, or "near me" with a **radius** (km) around a point |
| **Dates** | A from/to date range |
| **Child's age** | Shows only sessions whose age range fits (0–18) |
| **Category** | Activity type (e.g. sport, arts) |
| **Max price** | Hide anything above your budget |
| **Session length** | Half-day or full-day |
| **When** | Weekend, weekday, or after-school |
| **Available only** | On by default — hides sold-out sessions |

You can browse and search **without an account** — you only need to sign in when you're ready
to hold a seat and book.

### Quick feeds
Two curated shortcuts sit alongside search:
- **This weekend** — one-off activities running this Saturday/Sunday.
- **After school this week** — weekday after-school sessions.

### The listing page
Tap any result to open the **session detail** page. It shows the full description, venue,
date/time, **age range** (e.g. "5–8"), price, **live seats remaining**, reviews and rating,
and drop-off / what-to-bring details.

The seats-remaining number is **always live** — it reflects real availability at that moment,
never a stale or invented count.

### Ask a question (Q&A)
On a listing you can **ask the provider a question** (you'll need to be signed in). The
provider's answer is published on the listing so other parents benefit too. You can read all
existing questions and answers on the listing without signing in.

## 3. Booking & checkout

The flow is: **find a session → hold seats → cart → checkout → pay → booked.**

### Hold your seats
On a listing, choose how many seats you need and **hold** them. A hold is a short-term
reservation that stops someone else taking the seat while you finish booking.

> **Holds expire.** A browsing hold lasts about **15 minutes** by default. If it lapses before
> you check out, you'll be asked to **re-hold** — this is what keeps the seats-remaining count
> honest for everyone. There's also a cap on how many seats you can hold at once (to stop
> hoarding); if you hit it, release something or complete a booking first.

### Your cart
Held items collect in your **Cart** (the cart badge in the header shows the count). Your cart:
- can hold sessions from **multiple providers at once** — you check out **one cart, one
  payment**, even across different businesses;
- lets you **assign which child** goes into each held seat;
- totals up as price × number of children.

Your cart lives in your browser, so it's tied to the device you're using.

### Checkout
On **Checkout** you confirm each child and answer any per-session questions the provider asks
(e.g. dietary notes). A few rules are enforced here:

- **Age gating** — each child's age must fall **inside the session's age range**. A mismatch is
  blocked at checkout (this protects your child from an unsuitable activity and the provider
  from an out-of-range booking).
- **Child count must match** the seats you held.
- **Live availability** — seats are confirmed against real capacity; overselling is impossible.

### Paying
- If card payments are **live**, you'll pay securely with a card via the embedded payment form,
  and your booking confirms once payment settles (usually seconds).
- If the site is running in **demo mode** (no live card processing yet), checkout confirms
  immediately without a real charge.

Either way, the platform fee and its GST are calculated for you and included in the total —
you just see the final GST-inclusive price.

## 4. Managing your bookings

### My bookings
Avatar menu → **My bookings** (or the **My bookings** nav link) lists everything you've booked.
From here you can:
- see each session's date, time, venue and which child is going;
- **Add to calendar** — download an `.ics` file to drop the sessions into your calendar app.

### If a provider cancels
If a provider cancels a session, **every booking on it is automatically refunded in full** —
you don't need to chase anything. You'll be refunded to your original payment method.

> **Cancelling yourself:** there's no self-service "cancel my confirmed booking" button at the
> moment. If you haven't finished paying, you can abandon a pending payment. For a confirmed
> booking you need to change, contact the provider. (A parent-initiated cancellation/refund
> flow is on the roadmap.)

### Waitlists — for sold-out sessions
If a session is full, **join the waitlist**:
1. On a sold-out listing, choose **Join waitlist**.
2. Track your place under **My waitlist**.
3. If a seat frees up, you're **offered** it for a limited claim window. The offer comes with a
   ready-made hold — just go through the **normal checkout** to claim it before it expires.
4. You can **leave** a waitlist any time.

Only genuinely sold-out sessions can be waitlisted — if there are seats, you'll be told to just
book it.

### Reviews — after the activity
You can review a session **only after you've actually attended it** (a confirmed booking whose
session has finished). This keeps reviews trustworthy.
1. **My bookings** shows which past bookings are **eligible to review**.
2. Leave a **star rating** and a comment.
3. One review per booking. Please **don't include your child's name** in the text — the form
   will reject it to protect their privacy.

Providers can post a public reply to your review.

## 5. Holiday planner *(if enabled)*

The **Holiday planner** builds a suggested itinerary of activities across a date range for you.

1. Open **Holiday planner** (in the nav when signed in).
2. Give it a **date range** and any preferences.
3. It returns a suggested plan of sessions spread across the days.
4. **Add the whole plan to your cart** in one tap — it matches each session to a suitable
   child by age, holds the seats, and drops them in your cart. Then check out as usual.

Anything that doesn't fit (age mismatch, sold out) is simply skipped.

> This feature is switched on by the kidspots team; if you don't see it, it isn't enabled yet.

## 6. Birthday parties & the Party Planner *(if enabled)*

### Booking a party
Browse **Birthday parties** to see party packages, open a package for details, and book a
party slot.

### Party Planner — let the guests vote
Can't decide on the party? The **Party Planner** lets your invitees help pick:

**As the host:**
1. **Party votes** → create a plan and pick a theme.
2. Add up to a few **options** (party packages or free-text ideas) and set a **voting
   deadline**.
3. Customise the **invite page** — template, title, welcome message, avatar, and which widgets
   show (countdown, who's-coming board, etc.).
4. **Open** it for voting and **invite guests by email**, or just share the invite **link**.
5. When the deadline passes, **confirm the winning option and headcount** — this books the
   party and takes a deposit.

**As an invited guest (no account needed):**
1. Open the **invite link**.
2. Enter your name and email to **RSVP**.
3. **Tap an option to vote** — RSVP and vote happen in one step.
4. You can react to options and update your RSVP from your own guest link.

Invite pages are private (search engines are told not to index them), and we keep everything
**pseudonymous** — use nicknames, never a child's full name.

> The Party Planner is switched on deliberately by the kidspots team and depends on email being
> configured. If you don't see **Party votes**, it isn't enabled yet.

## Quick reference — the rules that protect you

- **Prices are GST-inclusive** — no surprise fees at the end.
- **Live availability** — seats-remaining is always real; no fake countdowns or scarcity.
- **Holds expire (~15 min)** — so counts stay honest; re-hold if yours lapses.
- **Age gating** — kids can only be booked into age-appropriate sessions.
- **One cart, one payment** — even across multiple providers.
- **Provider cancels ⇒ automatic full refund.**
- **Reviews only after attending**, one per booking, no child names.
- **Child data is minimal** — first name, DOB, medical/emergency contact only; never surnames
  or photos, and it's only visible to a provider your child is booked with.
