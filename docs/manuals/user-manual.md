# Juggle — Parent & Guardian Manual

**Who this is for:** parents and guardians using Juggle (kidspots.store) to find and book
kids' activities in and around Perth — school-holiday programs, weekend and after-school
one-offs, and birthday parties.

**How to read it:** §1–§4 is everything you need for your first booking. §5 onward covers
the features you'll meet later. Anything marked *(if enabled)* is switched on deliberately
by the Juggle team and may not be visible yet.

## 1. Getting started

### Creating an account

Two ways in, both from **Sign in**:

- **Email and password.** Give an email address and a password. That's the whole form —
  there's no email-verification step to wait for.
- **Phone code (SMS).** Enter your mobile number, we text you a one-time code, you type it
  in. If you've used Juggle before with that number, you land back in the same account. If
  you haven't, one is created for you.

> **Note on phone numbers:** signing up with email never attaches a phone number to your
> account. A number is only ever linked to you by actually receiving and entering a code on
> it — so nobody can claim your number by typing it into a signup form.

### Signing in later

Same two options. Sessions refresh themselves quietly in the background, so you generally
stay signed in. Signing out clears the session everywhere on that device.

### If you can't get in

There is currently **no "forgot password" email**. If you're locked out of a password
account, the practical route back in is the **phone code**, if your account has a verified
number. Otherwise contact support — an admin can help.

## 2. Your kids

**Account → My kids.**

Add each child once and reuse them on every booking. For each child we ask for:

| Field | Why |
| --- | --- |
| First name | So the provider knows who's arriving |
| Date of birth | To check the activity's age range — nothing else |
| Medical / allergy notes | Passed to the provider running the session |
| Emergency contact name and phone | Given to the provider for the day |

**That's the complete list.** We deliberately don't collect a child's surname or photo, and
there's no field for either — see §14.

Keep the date of birth accurate: age gating is enforced at booking time, not by eye, and an
age that doesn't match will block the booking.

## 3. Finding activities

### Search

The **Discover** page is the main search. You can filter by:

- **Suburb or postcode**, or tap **Use my location** for a radius search around you
  (default 25 km)
- **Dates** — a single day or a range
- **Child age** — shows only sessions whose age range fits
- **Category** — sport, art, STEM, drama and others
- **Price** and **session type** (e.g. full day)
- **Day of the week**, and **weekend / weekday / after-school** filters

### Quick feeds

Two shortcuts for "what's on soon":

- **This weekend** — one-off activities running this Saturday and Sunday
- **After school this week** — weekday sessions starting late enough for a school pickup

### What the listing tells you

Each session shows the provider, venue and suburb, the date and time, the price
(**GST-inclusive** — the number you see is the number you pay), the age range, and
**seats remaining**.

Seats remaining is always read live from real inventory. If it says 3 left, there are 3
left. We don't run countdown timers, "12 people are viewing this" counters, or any other
invented urgency.

### Holiday planner *(if enabled)*

**Planner** builds a whole-week suggestion from a short brief: which suburb, which dates,
your kids' ages, and optionally a budget. It returns a day-by-day plan drawn only from
sessions that genuinely have seats, and you can add the entire plan to your cart in one tap.
Anything that doesn't fit a child's age is skipped automatically.

## 4. Booking and paying

### How a booking is put together

1. **Hold a seat.** On a session, pick the child (or children) and hold the seat. The seat
   is now yours for a short window — **about 15 minutes** — so nobody can take it while you
   keep browsing.
2. **Add more if you like.** Holds accumulate in your **cart**. One cart can hold sessions
   from *different providers*, on different days.
3. **Check out once.** You pay a single total for the whole cart, however many providers it
   spans.

> **Holds expire.** That's what keeps seat counts honest for everyone. If yours lapses
> before you check out, just hold the seat again — you haven't lost anything unless someone
> else took the last seat in the meantime.
>
> There's also a cap on how many seats you can hold at once (8 by default), so nobody can
> park the entire calendar.

### Paying

Checkout shows an order summary and one total. Payment is by card. The price you were shown
is the price charged — GST is included and there's no card surcharge added at the end.

Once payment succeeds your booking is **confirmed** and you'll get a confirmation email.

> **If a seat is lost during payment** — very rare, but possible if two people pay for the
> last seat at the same moment — you are **automatically refunded** for that seat and told
> what happened. You are never charged for a seat you didn't get.

### Adding a booking to your calendar

Each order can be downloaded as a calendar file (`.ics`) and opened in Apple Calendar,
Google Calendar or Outlook, so the session lands in your family calendar with the right time
and venue.

## 5. Managing your bookings

**Bookings** lists everything you've booked, upcoming and past, with the provider, venue,
date, which child is attending, and what you paid.

### If the provider cancels

You are **automatically refunded in full** for that session. You don't need to ask, chase,
or fill in a form. In a multi-session order only the cancelled session is refunded — the
rest of your booking stands. You'll be emailed when it happens.

### If you need to cancel

Cancellation terms are the provider's, not ours. Contact the provider, or reach support —
an admin can issue a refund where it's warranted.

### What to bring

If the provider has specified what to bring, it appears on the booking, and we email it to
you automatically about **24 hours before** the session — along with reminders at roughly
**48 hours** and **3 hours** before.

## 6. When something sells out — waitlists

If a session is full you can **join the waitlist** from the session page.

When a seat frees up — someone cancels, or a hold lapses — the first person on the list is
**offered** it by email. The offer is yours to claim for a limited window (**4 hours** by
default). Claim it and you check out normally; miss it and the offer passes to the next
person.

**My waitlists** shows where you're waiting, and you can leave a waitlist at any time.

## 7. Birthday parties

**Birthday parties** lists party packages from local providers: a venue, a per-head price,
a guest range, and bookable dates.

### Booking a party

1. Open a package to see what's included, the price per guest, and the min/max guest count.
2. Pick a **date** — each date is one party, so once it's taken it's gone.
3. Set your **guest count** within the package's range.
4. Pay a **deposit** (30% by default) to lock the date in.

### The rest of the money

- The **balance** is charged automatically about **7 days before** the party, to the card
  you used for the deposit. You'll be emailed if that charge needs your attention.
- You can **adjust your guest count** up until roughly **72 hours** before the party. Going
  up charges the difference; going down refunds it.
- If the balance can't be charged after several attempts, the booking is cancelled and the
  provider is told, so nobody is left guessing.

### If the provider cancels your party

Full refund, **including the deposit** — always. An outdoor package may carry a weather
clause; that only ever means the party is **rescheduled**, never that you lose money.

## 8. Party votes — let guests choose *(if enabled)*

Can't decide between options? **Party votes** lets your invitees pick.

**As the host:**

1. **Party votes → new plan**, pick a theme.
2. Add up to **4 options** — real party packages, or free-text ideas ("backyard party at
   ours").
3. Optionally **rank** them: your ranking breaks ties.
4. Set a **voting deadline**.
5. Customise the **guest page** — template, title, welcome message, a sticker avatar, and
   which widgets show (countdown, who's-coming board, and so on).
6. **Open** it, then invite guests by email or just share the link.

**As an invited guest — no account needed:**

1. Open the invite link.
2. Enter your name and email to **RSVP**.
3. **Tap an option to vote** — RSVP and vote happen in one step.
4. React to options, and update your RSVP later from your own guest link.

**When the deadline passes**, the winner is announced to everyone who RSVP'd, and you book
it in one tap through the normal party deposit flow.

- If the vote **ties**, guests are told you'll decide. Once you pick, everyone is emailed
  your final choice.
- If you **override** a clear winner, guests get a change-of-plans notice.
- If you simply book the option that won, nobody gets a second email — they already know.

Invite pages are private (search engines are told not to index them) and everything stays
**pseudonymous**: use a nickname, never a child's full name.

## 9. Bespoke party quotes *(if enabled)*

For something the packages don't cover — an entertainer at your place, a custom setup — use
**Get a quote**.

1. Send a short **enquiry**: date and time, suburb, how many kids and their age range, an
   optional budget, and any notes. *(Please don't put kids' names in the notes.)*
2. The provider replies with **one price**, the time, and what's included.
3. You may ask for **one revision** — "could you do $45/head?" — and they may send **one**
   revised quote back. That's the deliberate limit: it keeps things clear instead of
   becoming a negotiation thread. There is no chat.
4. **Accept**, and you pay the same deposit as any party booking; the balance is charged
   later, exactly like an instant-book party.

Quotes **expire after 72 hours** by default, so prices and dates stay honest. If yours
lapses, send a fresh enquiry. You can withdraw an enquiry any time before you accept, and
the provider can decline or withdraw too — you'll be told either way.

## 10. Sharing a family — co-guardians

**Account → co-guardians.** Invite another adult by email to share your family: they see the
same kids and the same bookings, and can book for them.

- The person you invite **must already have a Juggle account** — ask them to sign up first.
- They accept the invitation from their own account.
- You can remove a co-guardian link at any time.

## 11. Reviews and questions

**Reviews.** Once a session you booked has finished, you can leave a rating and a comment —
one review per booking. Only confirmed bookings can review, so ratings mean something
rather than being open to anyone with an opinion. Providers can reply once.
Reviews are moderated — anything containing a child's name is screened out.

**Questions.** On a listing you can ask the provider a question publicly; their answer shows
on the listing for the next family with the same question.

## 12. Notifications and email

**Notifications** shows what we've sent you. You'll receive email for:

| When | What |
| --- | --- |
| You book | Booking confirmation |
| ~48h and ~3h before | Session reminders |
| ~24h before | What to bring |
| A seat frees up | Waitlist offer (time-limited) |
| Provider cancels | Cancellation and automatic refund |
| Seat lost at payment | Explanation and automatic refund |
| Party balance needs attention | Payment action needed |
| Party votes | Invitations, reveal, changes, nudges |
| Party quotes | Quote received, revised, accepted, expired or withdrawn |
| Signing in by phone | Your one-time code |

Providers can also send you a **broadcast** about a session you're booked into — these are
template-guarded and limited to the session at hand, not marketing.

## 13. Using Juggle on your phone

Juggle is a **progressive web app**: open kidspots.store in your phone browser and use
"Add to Home Screen" to install it like an app, with its own icon and offline shell.

On a phone the main destinations sit in a **bottom tab bar** — Discover, Parties, Bookings,
Cart and Account (Sign in if you're signed out) — with a badge on Cart when it has items.

## 14. Your privacy and your data

**What we collect about your child, in full:** first name, date of birth, medical/allergy
notes, and an emergency contact. Nothing else — there is deliberately no field for a
surname or a photo anywhere in Juggle.

**Where child details never go:** analytics, share links, invitations, party pages, guest
boards, or any public surface. Party pages and guest lists are pseudonymous by design —
nicknames and stickers, not names.

**Deleting your account.** **Account → delete account** permanently removes your personal
details and your children's. Your bookings and payment records are kept because they're
financial records, but they no longer carry personal information. **This cannot be undone**,
and afterwards the account can't be restored or reopened.

## 15. Troubleshooting

**"My hold expired while I was deciding."**
Expected — holds last about 15 minutes. Re-hold the seat. If it's now sold out, join the
waitlist (§6).

**"The seat count changed while I was looking."**
It's live. Someone booked or a hold lapsed. Refresh to see the current number.

**"I can't add my child to this session."**
Age gating. Check the child's date of birth on **My kids** and the session's age range.

**"I paid but my booking still says pending."**
Confirmation can take a few seconds while the payment settles. If it's still pending after
a minute or two, check **Bookings** again before retrying — you won't be charged twice, as
checkout is idempotent.

**"I didn't get an email."**
Check spam first. Email delivery has to be configured by the Juggle team — if the platform
is running without it, notifications are recorded in **Notifications** in the app even when
no email is sent.

**"I can't sign in and I don't remember my password."**
See §1 — there's no password-reset email yet; use the phone code if your account has a
verified number, otherwise contact support.

## 16. The promises we make you

- **Prices are GST-inclusive.** The price shown is the price charged; no surprise fees, no
  card surcharge.
- **Live availability.** Seats-remaining is always real. No fake countdowns, no invented
  scarcity, no "only 2 left!" unless there are exactly 2 left.
- **Holds expire (~15 min)** so counts stay honest for every family, including yours.
- **Age gating** — kids can only be booked into age-appropriate sessions.
- **One cart, one payment**, even across several providers.
- **Provider cancels ⇒ automatic full refund**, deposits included.
- **Minimal child data** — first name, DOB, medical notes and an emergency contact. Never a
  surname or a photo, and never in analytics or on a public page.
